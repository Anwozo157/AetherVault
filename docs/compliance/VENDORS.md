## Subservice organizations for the SOC 2 report

For the audit, the critical vendors are treated as **carved-out subservice organizations**: the
auditor relies on their own SOC 2 reports, and our report lists the controls we expect them to run
(see `SYSTEM_DESCRIPTION.md`). That makes the Supabase, GitHub and Cloudflare reports required
evidence. Resend becomes one if the auditor counts sign-in email as in scope.
