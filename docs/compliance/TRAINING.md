# Security Awareness Training

Required on joining (before access to customer data) and yearly. Free resources are fine; keep a
record of completion.

## Curriculum (about 2 hours a year)

1. **Our policies:** read every policy in `docs/policies/` and sign the acknowledgement table.
2. **Phishing and account takeover:** recognizing phishing, MFA, password managers, what to do if you
   clicked.
3. **Handling secrets and customer data:** `docs/policies/data-management.md`; never paste secrets
   into chat or AI tools.
4. **Incident reporting:** how and when to report (`docs/policies/incident-response.md`).
5. **Secure development** (engineers): OWASP Top 10 basics, our CSP and RLS patterns, dependency hygiene.

Free sources for parts 2–5: the U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency's (CISA)
small-business and Cyber Essentials materials, the FTC's "Cybersecurity for Small Business" guides,
and the OWASP Top 10. Record which ones you used.
